# Design

This section translates the approved strategy and discovery processes into desktop-first, low-fidelity Salesforce experiences.

## Artifacts

- [Salesforce wireframes](02-wireframes/README.md) — ten screens covering fan identity, duplicates, intake, Campaigns, revenue, tier/lapse, membership/VIP, Cases, dashboards, and the Home workspace.

## Design authority

- [Org strategy](../01-strategy/01-org-strategy.md) controls architecture and scope.
- [Implementation plan](../01-strategy/02-implementation-plan.md) controls functional outcomes and phases.
- [Discovery process maps](../02-discovery/03-business-process-maps/README.md) control future-state business flow.
- [Admin runbook](../01-strategy/03-admin-runbook.md) controls operating, privacy, testing, and maintenance behavior.

The wireframes are structural design references, not deployed Lightning metadata or rendered application QA.
