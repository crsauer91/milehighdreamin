# Design

This section translates approved strategy and discovery into implementation-ready solution architecture and desktop-first, low-fidelity Salesforce experiences.

## Artifacts

- [Solution design](01-solution-design.md) — record relationships, intake paths, revenue mechanics, duplicate and exception persistence, Case design, tier history, and reporting dependencies.
- [Salesforce wireframes](02-wireframes/README.md) — ten principal operational experiences covering fan identity, duplicates, intake, Campaigns, revenue, tier/lapse, membership/VIP, Cases, dashboards, and Home.

## Design authority

- [Org strategy](../01-strategy/01-org-strategy.md) controls architecture and scope.
- [Implementation plan](../01-strategy/02-implementation-plan.md) controls functional outcomes and phases.
- [Decision register](../01-strategy/05-decision-register.md) controls approved architecture and business rules.
- [Discovery process maps](../02-discovery/03-business-process-maps/README.md) control future-state business flow.
- [Controlled values](../02-discovery/04-execution-framework/02-controlled-values.md) control reportable values and lifecycles.
- [Field mappings](../02-discovery/04-execution-framework/03-field-mappings.md) control fields and relationships.
- [KPI dictionary](../02-discovery/04-execution-framework/06-kpi-dictionary.md) controls metric meaning.
- [Admin runbook](../01-strategy/03-admin-runbook.md) controls operating, privacy, testing, and maintenance behavior.

The solution design explains implementation mechanics without duplicating governing sources. Wireframes are structural references, not deployed Lightning metadata or rendered application QA.
