# Client Brief: Crowdfunding First

## Revenue Logic

Campaign-based funding, tiered backers, and project launches drive the business.

## Salesforce Priorities

- Campaign
- Campaign Member
- Opportunity
- Contact

## Core Flows

- Campaign Member Follow-Up
- Fan Tier Evaluation

## Key KPIs

- Backer conversion
- Repeat backing
- Revenue by campaign
- Post-campaign retention

## Recommended Constraint

Use Campaigns heavily and avoid a custom crowdfunding schema in the MVP.
